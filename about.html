About Microfiber Bay

An independent guide to socks, created for anyone who has ever wondered why one pair feels perfect and another leaves their feet tired by lunchtime.

Our story

It started with a drawer of mismatched socks

Microfiber Bay began when we realised how little most of us know about something we wear every single day. Why did the thin running socks dry overnight while the cotton pair stayed damp? Why did some socks last years and others wear through in weeks?

Answering those questions meant learning about fibres, knitting, sizing and care. This website gathers what we learned, explained clearly and without sales talk.

Independent

We do not sell socks and are not affiliated with any brand, manufacturer or retailer.

Practical

Every guide aims to help you make a real decision, from choosing a fibre to fixing a slipping heel.

Plain language

We explain technical terms simply and point out where personal preference matters most.

How we work

Research before recommendations

Our guides draw on textile references, published fibre information, care-label standards and conversations with knitters, runners and hikers. We describe how materials generally behave rather than ranking products.
