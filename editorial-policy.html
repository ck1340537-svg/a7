Editorial Policy

The standards behind every Microfiber Bay guide.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Readers rely on Microfiber Bay for clear, accurate information. These standards guide everything we publish.

Independence

We do not accept payment, free products or gifts in exchange for coverage, and our guides are brand-neutral. Advertisers, if present, have no influence over our content.

Research

We draw on textile references, published fibre data, care-labelling standards and the experience of knitters, runners and hikers. Technical facts are checked against recognised sources.

Responsible writing

  • We describe how fibres generally behave rather than making absolute performance claims.
  • We do not make medical or therapeutic claims about socks.
  • We explain technical terms in plain language.
  • We note where personal preference and fit matter most.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please tell us through our contact page. We review every report, correct confirmed mistakes promptly and update the page date for significant changes.

Images and tools

Photographs are sourced from Unsplash under the Unsplash License and described with accurate alternative text. Interactive tools are created by us and offer general guidance only.
